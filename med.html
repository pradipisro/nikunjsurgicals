About Us

Crafting Surgical Excellence Since 1989

Nikunj Surgicals is a globally recognized manufacturer of premium surgical instruments. Founded in Tuttlingen, Germany — the heart of medical instrument manufacturing — we combine centuries-old craftsmanship with cutting-edge technology to produce instruments that meet the exacting standards of modern surgery.

Every instrument undergoes rigorous quality control testing, ensuring consistent performance, durability, and patient safety across thousands of procedures worldwide.
